Privacy Notice

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Field Web Design respects your privacy. This notice explains what personal information we may receive when you contact us about our website design services, why we use it and how we look after it.

Information we may receive

When you contact us through the enquiry form or by email, you may provide information such as your name, business name, email address and details about the website or services you are interested in.

How we use your information

We use information you provide to respond to your enquiry, discuss your requirements, prepare or provide our services, and communicate with you about your project.

How we receive enquiries

The website enquiry form opens your email application to send an email to jcfielddesign@outlook.com. Information included in that email is handled through the email service you use and our business email account.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information where necessary to provide a service you have requested, to operate our business, or where we are required to do so by law.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including any legitimate business, accounting or legal requirements.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights including access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, objection to certain processing, and deletion where applicable.

If you have a question about how your information is used, please contact jcfielddesign@outlook.com.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
